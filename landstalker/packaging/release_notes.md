**Project-Friday {VERSION} - Landstalker (Mega Drive) recompiled for Steam Deck / Linux and Windows.**

### New in {VERSION}
- **Native Pace is now on by default:** busy rooms (e.g. the swamp shrine) no longer run in slow motion. Switch it off in Options > Quality of Life for the exact console timing.
- **Modern HUD (new option, Options > Video > HUD, default Original):** no status bar; hearts and the sword charge top left, gold bottom right, round buttons top right with the sword and the Quick Items (with the name of the bound button, follows your key configuration). In the Start menu only the hearts are shown.
- **Quick Equip, new control:** hold R2 and press a direction to open the strip of swords (up), armour (right), boots (down) or rings (left), then choose with left / right.
- **Item icons straight from the game:** Quick Items, Quick Equip and the HUD buttons show every item right away (no visit to the Start menu needed first).
- Everything from v1.3.0 is included.
